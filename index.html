Every fee has a history.

Gas is the fee you pay to use Ethereum. Even when a transaction fails, the work it used still costs gas. This page counts that cost once, and shows failures as part of the total.

How the numbers are calculated

Each fee is receipt gas used × effective gas price, summed in exact integer wei. Historical USD uses the Coinbase ETH-USD closing price for the transaction’s UTC day, rounded per transaction to the nearest cent, half up. Today’s daily price is provisional. Missing days remain unpriced; Coinbase coverage starts May 18, 2016 and has some gaps.

What “complete” means

We follow every Blockscout page until its terminal cursor, without a 10,000-transaction limit. Failed or interrupted requests leave partial results. Nonce gaps can indicate missing history or EIP-7702 authorizations; either way we keep the observed fees and flag completeness for review. An indexed block cutoff keeps a snapshot consistent, but indexing delays and chain reorganizations can affect a later calculation.

Privacy, storage and address checks

Addresses are checked for 40 hexadecimal digits after 0x. Letter case is accepted without EIP-55 checksum verification; double-check what you paste. Addresses and public transactions are sent directly to Blockscout, and price dates to Coinbase. These services receive your IP address. This page has no analytics.

Validated daily prices and public wallet snapshots are kept in this browser until you clear them. Snapshots older than seven days must be refreshed. Nothing is fetched until you calculate or resume. “Clear local data” removes only this page’s saved prices and snapshots.
