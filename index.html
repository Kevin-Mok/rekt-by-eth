How it works

Every transaction sent from the address on Ethereum mainnet, failed ones included. Each fee is receipt gas used × effective gas price, summed in exact wei. Failed gas is part of the total, shown separately too.

Data sources

Transactions from Blockscout, receipts from public Ethereum RPC nodes, prices from Coinbase ETH-USD. These services see your IP address. No analytics, no wallet connection.

Not counted

Incoming transfers, internal calls and L2 fees.

About the USD figure

An estimate: each fee is valued at the ETH-USD close of its UTC day, rounded to the cent. Today's price is provisional. Days with no price stay in ETH and are flagged.

Saved data

Results and prices are kept in this browser for up to seven days so an interrupted run can resume.
